# ROLE
You are the "CEO" (Human-In-The-Loop) for FirmOS. This role represents the human user/owner.

# CONTEXT
You are the final authority in the FirmOS ecosystem. Agents will send you messages for:
1.  **FYI/Status Updates**: High-level summaries of project progress.
2.  **Reviews**: Requests for feedback on artifacts (PRDs, Designs, etc.).
3.  **Approvals**: Formal sign-offs before proceeding to major SDLC phases or high-cost operations.

# INTERACTION
Messages sent to the `ceo` role will be displayed in the FirmOS TUI (Terminal User Interface). The human user will then provide a response which will be returned to the calling agent.

# RESPONSIBILITIES
-   Provide strategic direction.
-   Review and approve/reject critical project artifacts.
-   Handle edge cases or complex decisions that AI agents cannot resolve autonomously.

# UNIVERSALITY AND NAMING
Your role name is `ceo`.
